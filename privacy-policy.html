2. Information We Collect

a. Phone Number

We collect your mobile phone number for OTP-based authentication. This is the primary method of verifying your identity when you create an account or sign in.

b. Vendor GPS Location

If you register as a vendor, we collect your real-time GPS location to enable live location broadcast to nearby customers. Location data is collected while the vendor actively broadcasts their availability through the app.

c. Customer Locality Information

If you register as a customer, we collect your locality information to match you with nearby vendors and display relevant vendor availability in your area.

d. Profile Information

We collect your name and other profile details you provide when setting up your account.

e. Transaction Data

We collect data related to coin purchases, coin usage, and payment transactions processed through our payment partners. This includes transaction amounts, timestamps, and payment confirmation details.

f. Usage Data

We automatically collect information about how you interact with the app, including:

  • App interaction data (screens viewed, features used, actions taken)
  • Device information (device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers)
  • Log data (access times, error logs, referring pages)

g. Ratings and Reviews

We collect ratings and reviews that you submit about vendors or the service, which may be displayed publicly within the app.

h. Communications

We collect any messages or communications you send to us through in-app support, email, or other channels.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Phone Number: To authenticate your identity via OTP, send transactional notifications, and communicate important account-related information.
  • Vendor GPS Location: To enable real-time location broadcast so customers can find nearby vendors, and to facilitate vendor-customer coordination.
  • Customer Locality: To display relevant vendors in your area and personalise your experience based on your neighbourhood.
  • Profile Information: To identify you within the app and provide a personalised experience.
  • Transaction Data: To process coin purchases, manage your coin balance, facilitate payments through our payment partners, and maintain financial records as required by law.
  • Usage Data: To improve app performance, diagnose technical issues, understand usage patterns, and enhance user experience.
  • Ratings and Reviews: To maintain service quality, provide feedback to vendors, and help customers make informed choices.
  • Communications: To respond to your inquiries, resolve support requests, and improve our services.

7. Your Rights Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

Consent

By using the {{ site.data.site.app_name }} app, you consent to the collection and processing of your personal data as described in this Privacy Policy. You may withdraw your consent at any time, subject to the provisions of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

Your Rights as a Data Principal

Under the DPDP Act, 2023, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: You have the right to obtain a summary of your personal data being processed and the processing activities undertaken with that data.
  • Right to Correction: You have the right to request correction of inaccurate or misleading personal data, and completion of incomplete personal data.
  • Right to Erasure: You have the right to request erasure of your personal data that is no longer necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You have the right to withdraw your consent for data processing at any time. You can do this through in-app settings or by contacting us.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: You have the right to register a grievance with our Grievance Officer regarding any data processing concerns.
  • Right to Nominate: You have the right to nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.

Response Timeline

We will respond to your requests within 90 days of receipt, as stipulated under the DPDP Act, 2023.

Data Protection Board

If you are not satisfied with our response to your grievance, you have the right to file a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India established under the DPDP Act, 2023.
