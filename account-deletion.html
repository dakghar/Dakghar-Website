Option 2: Request Deletion by Email

Please include the following in your email:

  • Your registered phone number
  • Reason for deletion (optional)

What Gets Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data will be permanently removed:

  • Profile information
  • Phone number
  • Vendor/customer preferences
  • Transaction history
  • Ratings and reviews you submitted
  • Coin balance (forfeited)

What May Be Retained

Certain data may be retained to comply with legal obligations:

  • Transaction records (as required by Indian financial regulations)
  • Security logs (retained for {{ site.data.legal.data_retention.security_logs_days }} days per DPDP Rules 2025)
  • Aggregated, anonymized data that cannot identify you

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are processed within {{ site.data.legal.account_deletion.processing_time }}.

Note: Once deletion is processed, it cannot be reversed. Any remaining coin balance will be forfeited.
